# Reporting a security vulnerability

Please **do not open a public issue** for a vulnerability in LakehouseBox.

Report it privately, either way:

- **GitHub**: [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/lakehousebox/lakehousebox/security/advisories/new) (a private
  advisory only we can see).
- **Email**: [hello@lakehousebox.com](mailto:hello@lakehousebox.com).

Tell us what you found, how to reproduce it and what an attacker could reach with it. You may test your own catalogs
and credentials; please do not test other customers' data or load-test the shared service without asking first.
We do not act against good-faith research that follows these rules.

The security overview of the service: <https://lakehousebox.com/legal/security/>. Our contact is also published in
<https://lakehousebox.com/.well-known/security.txt>.
